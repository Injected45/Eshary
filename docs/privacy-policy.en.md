# Privacy Policy — Eshary app

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The "Eshary" app is provided by Al-Rahala Software Company ("we"). This policy explains what data we collect, how we use and protect it, and how you can delete it.
Contact about privacy or deletion: {{SUPPORT}}

## What we collect
- Registration data: the manager's name, the business name and a WhatsApp phone number, with your explicit consent to be contacted on WhatsApp about your request.
- Account data: an internal account identifier, and the status and dates of your trial or subscription.
- Business data you enter yourself: accounts, companies, clients, beneficiaries, outgoing and incoming operations, balances and branches.
- Employee data entered by the manager: name, phone number, permissions and their activity log inside the app.
- Technical data: a key derived (hashed) from the Android ID of an employee's phone, used only to bind that employee's account to one phone (the raw ID is never sent and is never used for advertising); an error log kept on your device; and a hashed fingerprint of the network address used to prevent abuse (the address itself is not stored).

We do not collect your location, contacts, messages or files. The camera is used only to scan a QR code; images are not saved.

## How we use it
To provide the service, to verify your number with a code sent through WhatsApp, to manage trials and subscriptions, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to send work alerts. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

## Sharing
Data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase). Verification codes are sent through WhatsApp (Meta), which processes your phone number and the message in line with its own policy. We share your data with no one else unless the law requires it.

## Security
Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Each account can reach only its own data, enforced in the database. Verification codes are stored hashed with a secret key, never as text. You can turn on a fingerprint lock in Settings.

## Retention and deletion
We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Settings, then "Delete my account"; a code sent to your registered number confirms that you are the owner.
On deletion we immediately remove the account and everything it owns (companies, accounts, clients, beneficiaries, operations, employees, branches) and the trial record, erase the personal content of the audit log (the fact that an event happened remains, without personal data), and remove your rows from the stored backups.
You can also ask for deletion without the app: {{SUPPORT}}

## Your rights
You may ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you at any time, from the app or by contacting us.

## Children
The app is for businesses and adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.

## Changes
We will tell you inside the app about any material change before it takes effect.
